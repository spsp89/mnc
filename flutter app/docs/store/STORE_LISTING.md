## Short description

Find trusted nearby businesses, products, services, offers and local opportunities.

## Full description

BNC helps people discover and connect with trusted businesses close to them.

Use BNC to:

- find businesses, products and professional services near your selected location;
- compare verified profiles, BNC plan stars, reviews and current offers;
- send a private enquiry and continue the conversation inside BNC;
- book participating clinics, doctors, salons and beauty services;
- discover local jobs and apply through the platform;
- manage orders, appointment updates and notifications from your account.

Location is optional. BNC asks for precise location only when you choose to see distance-based results. You can always select a city manually.
